I built this because I wanted it myself.

I was already keeping track of my schedule and trying to figure out what OT, DT, pickups, and everything else were going to do to my check. I wanted one place that handled both, without having to enter the same schedule over and over.

ShiftComp is what I ended up building. I'm testing it with other Ground Ops employees now, so if something doesn't work, or the numbers just look wrong, let me know.

Install it like an app

ShiftComp runs from your browser, but you can add it to your home screen and use it like a normal app. Once installed, it also works offline.

On Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen"
  3. Tap Add. It'll open like a normal app from there

On iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari
  2. Tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap Add. It'll open like a normal app from there

Before you start: grab a recent pay stub if you have one. A regular one works best, without a lot of overtime or extra hours mixed in. Setup asks a few questions about your pay and deductions.
